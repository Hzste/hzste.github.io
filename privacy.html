Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Clipworth ("Clipworth", "we", "us") collects when you use our Discord bot and related services, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Clipworth you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Clipworth is a service that lets video editors ("editors") earn money for the views their TikTok edits receive while taking part in campaigns run for streamers and other campaign owners. The service runs primarily through a Discord bot. You can reach us by joining our Discord and opening a ticket.

2. Information we collect

  • Discord information. When you interact with our bot we store your Discord user ID and username so we can recognise you, assign roles, and track your submissions and balance.
  • TikTok information (with your consent). When you connect a TikTok account, either by adding a verification code to your bio or by authorising through TikTok's official login, we store your TikTok username or handle and account identifier. Where you authorise it through TikTok's API, we also read public information about the videos you submit, including their view counts and related public metrics, to calculate your earnings. We only access TikTok data for accounts and videos you choose to connect or submit.
  • Submissions. The TikTok video links you submit, the campaign they belong to, and the resulting views and earnings.
  • Payment information. The PayPal email address you provide so we can pay you. We do not collect or store your PayPal password or financial credentials.
  • Operational data. Basic logs and timestamps used to run the service, prevent abuse and debug issues.

We do not knowingly collect any data beyond what is needed to run campaigns and pay creators.

3. How we use your information

  • To verify that a submitted edit was posted by an account you own.
  • To read your edits' view counts and calculate the earnings owed to you.
  • To manage campaigns, approvals, roles and balances.
  • To pay you via PayPal.
  • To detect fraud, fake or inorganic views, and abuse of the service.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the service.

4. How we use TikTok data specifically

When you connect TikTok through its official API, we use the access you grant only to confirm you own the account and to read view counts and public metrics for the videos you submit. We do not post on your behalf, we do not sell your TikTok data, and we handle it in line with TikTok's Developer Terms and Community Guidelines. You can revoke our access at any time (see "Your choices").

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Campaign owners (streamers and admins). The streamer or admin a campaign is run for can see the edits you submit to their campaign, your TikTok handle, the view counts of those edits, and the earnings attributed to them, so submissions can be reviewed and approved.
  • Service providers. Infrastructure we rely on to run the service (for example hosting and our database), and PayPal when we send you a payout.
  • Legal. Where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Clipworth and its users.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, calculate and record payouts, and meet legal or accounting obligations. When you remove a TikTok account, unverify, or leave, we delete the related submissions that have not yet been paid. You can ask us to delete your data entirely (see below).

7. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect TikTok. You can unlink a TikTok account from within Discord at any time, and you can revoke Clipworth's access from your TikTok account settings.
  • Access and deletion. You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by joining our Discord and opening a ticket.
  • Correction. You can update your linked accounts and PayPal email at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and limit access to what is necessary to run the service.

9. Children

Clipworth is not directed to children. You must meet TikTok's minimum age requirement and be old enough to enter a contract and receive payment in your country to use the service.

10. International users

Clipworth may process and store information in countries other than your own. By using the service you consent to this transfer, subject to the protections described here.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify users in our Discord.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us by joining our Discord and opening a ticket.

Clipworth is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or PayPal.
